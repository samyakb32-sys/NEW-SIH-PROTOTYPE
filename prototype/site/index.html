The Core Problem

The Real Challenge Indian Farmers Face

Farmers often lose 15% to 30% of their potential income — not because of poor yield, but due to information asymmetry at the point of sale.

Opaque Mandi Prices

Prices vary wildly between nearby mandis without clear net margin visibility.

Zero Price Visibility

Farmers are forced to sell immediately without knowing if prices will surge in 3–5 days.

Hidden Transport & Storage Costs

A higher gross offer 100km away often results in a lower net pocket realization.

Buyer Reliability & Payment Delays

Fear of delayed payment or unfair deductions at the buyer's gate.
