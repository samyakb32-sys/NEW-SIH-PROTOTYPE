The Platform

Everything between harvest and payment

Six systems, one flow — price discovery, AI recommendation, buyer matching, contracts, logistics and settlement, all tied to a single lot.

01

Real-Time Price Board

Live prices pulled from multiple mandis, compared instantly so no one sells blind to a middleman's number.

02

AI Recommendation

Price trend forecasts, a best-sale-window, and a net-return calculator that nets out transport and storage.

03

Verified Buyer Network

Buyer discovery scored by crop, quantity, quality grade and distance — not just who shouts loudest.

04

Digital Contracts

Deal terms locked and generated as a digital agreement the moment an offer is accepted.

05

Logistics Coordination

Compare transporters on cost, route and timing, then book pickup without a phone call.

06

Payment & Records

Invoice, payment status and a full transaction ledger with digital receipts, end to end.
